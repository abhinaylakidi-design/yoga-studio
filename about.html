About Zenora

A quiet place to come home to yourself.

For over a decade Zenora has been a sanctuary for people who need to pause, breathe and begin again. This is who we are, and why we do what we do.

Our Story

It began with a single mat and a deep breath

Zenora started in 2015 as a small room, a handful of mats and a simple belief: everyone deserves a place to slow down. Our founders, long-time practitioners, wanted a studio that felt less like a gym and more like a exhale.

Today that single room has grown into a thriving community, but our heart has not changed. Every class, every teacher and every corner of the studio is shaped around care, patience and presence.

The Zenora Founders Banjara Hills, Hyderabad
Purpose

What guides everything we do

Two simple promises that shape every class, conversation and decision at Zenora.

01

Our Mission

To make mindful movement and meditation welcoming, practical and accessible, so that people of every age, body and background can find steadiness in a busy world.

  • Judgement-free classes for every level
  • Safe, thoughtful and personal guidance

Our Vision

To be the city's most trusted home for well-being, a place where a calmer mind, a stronger body and a kinder community grow together for generations.

  • A community built on kindness
  • Well-being that lasts beyond the mat
