Your First Class

New to Zenora? It is simpler than you think

We handle the details so you can simply show up and breathe. Here is how a first visit works.

  1. 1

    Choose a class

    Pick a beginner-friendly session from the finder or timetable.

  2. 2

    Reserve your spot

    Book online in seconds and receive an instant confirmation.

  3. 3

    Arrive 10 minutes early

    Meet your teacher, settle in and tell us about any injuries.

  4. 4

    Practice and unwind

    Mats, props and tea are on us. Just bring water and comfy clothes.

What to bring

  • Water bottle
  • Comfortable clothing
  • Small towel (optional)
  • An open mind
