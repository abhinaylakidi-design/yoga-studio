Our Studio

A sanctuary for body, breath and mind

Zenora was created for people who need a pause. Our studio is softly lit, naturally scented and intentionally uncluttered, so you can leave the noise of the day at the door.

Whether you are stepping onto a mat for the first time or deepening a long-time practice, you will find a gentle, judgement-free community here.
