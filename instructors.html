Our Standards

How we choose and grow our teachers

Great teaching is earned. Every instructor meets the same high bar before they lead a class.

01

Properly certified

A minimum 200-hour registered training, with senior teachers holding 300 and 500-hour credentials.

02

Observed and mentored

New teachers shadow and are mentored by senior instructors before leading public classes.

03

Always learning

Ongoing workshops in anatomy, mindfulness, first aid and inclusive teaching every year.
